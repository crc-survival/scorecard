Colorectal Cancer Scorecard

Points-based risk groups and estimated overall survival for patients alive 3 months after diagnosis

Research prototype for researchers, not for clinical decisions or for use by patients. The scorecard was developed on patients diagnosed with colorectal cancer in 2018 in the Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results (SEER) cancer registries of the United States. In a first external evaluation, in 99 patients from a hospital in Thailand, it ranked patients by risk well but overestimated survival; further external validation is needed. Surgery and chemotherapy are inputs, so it applies once the first course of treatment is known, and its estimates are for patients alive 3 months after diagnosis. Estimates describe groups of similar patients, not an individual prognosis.
